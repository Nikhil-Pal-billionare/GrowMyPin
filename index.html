Questions

Answers before you start.

Do I need to share my Pinterest password?

No. You add us through Pinterest's official Business Manager with publisher access. You keep full ownership and can remove our access at any time.

When will I start seeing results?

Most accounts see initial traction within 2 to 3 weeks of optimized posting. Pinterest needs time to understand and index new content, and results depend on your niche, product quality and consistency.

Do you guarantee sales?

No. Pinterest drives discovery and traffic to your store. Sales depend on your listing, pricing and offer. We focus on building consistent, qualified traffic.

Can I choose which countries see my pins?

We target and optimize for the right audience and geography as far as possible, but Pinterest's algorithm makes the final distribution decision.

What happens after the free trial?

If you like what you see, a Rs 999 base charge starts your first month, with Rs 4,000 due at the end of month 1. From month 2, you pay Rs 999 per month plus milestone charges only when your views reach the next level.

What do I need to share each week?

Every Sunday, your Pinterest analytics export (CSV) and your store's views, visits and sales. This keeps our reporting accurate and lets us link Pinterest activity to real store results.
